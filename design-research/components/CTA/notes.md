## Example

**Name:** Scholar Match

**URL:** https://www.scholarmatch.org/about

**Screenshot:** announcement-in-navigation-bar.png

### Problem Solved

Keeps an important announcement or campaign highly visible without interrupting the user's browsing experience.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Announcement bar positioned above the navigation
- Clear, concise messaging
- Simple call-to-action
- CTA remains visible immediately upon page load

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Use an announcement bar for upcoming events or community initiatives.
- Highlight volunteer opportunities or important campaigns.
- Keep messaging short and action-oriented.

## Example

**Name:** Grouped CTA Buttons

**URL:** N/A

**Screenshot:** grouped-cta-buttons.png

### Problem Solved

Helps visitors quickly choose between two primary actions without overwhelming them.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Balanced primary and secondary CTA layout
- Clear visual hierarchy between actions
- Consistent button styling
- Easy-to-scan button labels

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Primary CTA: Join the Mission
- Secondary CTA: Donate or Volunteer
- Maintain clear visual distinction so one action leads the page.

## Example

**Name:** Contact Form CTA

**URL:** N/A

**Screenshot:** short-contact-form-on-page.png

### Problem Solved

Provides an easy way for visitors to take action once they've learned about the organization, reducing the chance they'll leave without reaching out.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Short, low-friction contact form
- Strong visual contrast between form and supporting imagery
- Inviting, approachable layout
- Clean section design with supporting photography

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Keep the contact form short (name, email, message).
- Consider placing it near the end of key pages.
- Pair the form with community photography to reinforce trust.

## Example

**Name:** Newsletter Signup Popup

**URL:** https://www.scholarmatch.org

**Screenshot:** newsletter-popup.png

### Problem Solved

Creates an opportunity for visitors to stay connected even if they aren't ready to volunteer or donate today.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Simple email-only signup
- Minimal copy
- Clear value proposition
- Clean popup design

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Offer newsletter signup to keep supporters engaged.
- Test whether a popup or inline signup performs better.
- Explain the benefit of subscribing (events, community updates, opportunities to get involved).
