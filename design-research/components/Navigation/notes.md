# Navigation Design Research Notes

---

## Example

**Name:** Goodwill Navigation

**URL:** https://goodwill-template.webflow.io/

**Screenshot:** goodwill-navigation.png

### Problem Solved

Makes it easy for visitors to navigate the website while keeping the organization's primary call-to-action visible and accessible at all times.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Simple navigation layout with the logo on the left and navigation links on the right.
- Large, highly visible primary CTA immediately draws attention.
- Minimal navigation items reduce cognitive load and make the interface easier to scan.
- Clear spacing between navigation elements improves readability and usability.
- The CTA visually stands apart from the navigation links without overpowering the overall design.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Keep the navigation simple and focused on the organization's most important pages.
- Include a prominent primary CTA such as **"Join the Mission"** or **"Donate"** that remains visible across the site.
- Limit the number of navigation items to keep the experience clean and intuitive.
- Ensure the navigation feels welcoming and approachable while reinforcing the organization's brand.
