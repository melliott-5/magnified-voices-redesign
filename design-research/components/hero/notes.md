## Example

**Name:** Care Hands Hero

**URL:** https://care-hands.webflow.io/

**Screenshot:** care-hands-hero-img.png

### Problem Solved

Immediately communicates the organization's mission by answering the "Big 3" within the first few seconds: who they are, what they do, and who they serve.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Strong visual hierarchy guides the visitor's attention from the community imagery, to the headline, and finally to the primary call-to-action.
- Authentic photography creates an immediate emotional connection and reinforces the mission.
- The hero clearly answers the "Big 3" without requiring users to scroll.
- A unique secondary CTA treatment helps highlight volunteer opportunities without competing with the primary action.
- Clean spacing allows each element to breathe and improves readability.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Answer the "Big 3" within the first five seconds.
- Lead with one authentic community-focused image that represents the mission.
- Use a clear primary CTA ("Join the Mission") supported by a secondary CTA ("Donate" or "Volunteer").
- Create a strong visual hierarchy that naturally guides visitors from the imagery to the message and then to the desired action.

## Example

**Name:** Equalize

**URL:** https://equalize.webflow.io/#About

**Screenshot:** equalize-hero-img.png

### Problem Solved

Introduces the organization with a strong visual identity while clearly communicating its mission and providing multiple paths for visitors to take action.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Brand illustrations and iconography reinforce the organization's personality.
- Balanced primary and secondary CTAs provide clear choices without overwhelming visitors.
- Strong visual hierarchy makes the hero easy to scan.
- Supporting graphics enhance the brand without distracting from the messaging.
- Consistent button styling creates a polished, cohesive experience.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Use subtle supporting graphics or iconography to strengthen the visual identity.
- Include a primary CTA ("Join the Mission") and a clearly differentiated secondary CTA ("Donate" or "Volunteer").
- Maintain a simple, easy-to-scan layout with concise messaging.
- Ensure the hero establishes trust while communicating the organization's purpose.

## Example

**Name:** Fundraising WCopilot Hero

**URL:** https://fundraising-wcopilot.webflow.io/home-2

**Screenshot:** fundraising-hero.png

### Problem Solved

Creates a compelling first impression by combining strong messaging, thoughtful spacing, and supporting content that reinforces the organization's mission and goals.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Generous whitespace improves readability and visual balance.
- Clear hierarchy separates the headline, supporting copy, CTAs, and supporting content.
- Supporting sections within the hero provide additional context without overwhelming the visitor.
- Background imagery reinforces the mission while maintaining focus on the messaging.
- Consistent spacing creates a premium, well-crafted experience.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Use generous spacing to create a calm and approachable experience.
- Consider introducing a supporting "Our Impact" or "Our Mission" highlight within or immediately below the hero.
- Keep supporting copy concise while reinforcing the organization's purpose.
- Use imagery that supports the mission without competing with the headline or primary CTA.
