## Example

**Name:** Sticky Navigation

**URL:** Various

**Screenshot:** sticky-navigation.png

### Problem Solved

Keeps important navigation and primary actions accessible as visitors explore the website, reducing the need to scroll back to the top.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Sticky navigation remains visible during scrolling.
- Smooth transition between navigation states.
- Primary CTA remains accessible.
- Minimal visual distraction.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Keep the navigation visible while scrolling.
- Ensure the primary CTA ("Join the Mission" or "Donate") is always accessible.
- Use subtle transitions that feel smooth and professional.

## Example

**Name:** Button Hover States

**URL:** Various

**Screenshot:** button-hover.png

### Problem Solved

Provides immediate visual feedback that an element is interactive, increasing confidence before users click.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Subtle background color transition.
- Slight elevation or shadow on hover.
- Consistent hover behavior across all buttons.
- Smooth transition timing.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Use consistent hover states throughout the website.
- Reinforce the organization's approachable personality with soft, subtle animations.
- Avoid overly dramatic hover effects.

## Example

**Name:** Card Hover Interaction

**URL:** Various

**Screenshot:** card-hover.png

### Problem Solved

Encourages visitors to explore programs, resources, and stories by making interactive content feel responsive.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Slight image zoom.
- Soft shadow increase.
- Smooth movement.
- Consistent animation across all cards.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Apply hover interactions to program cards, resource cards, and featured stories.
- Keep animations subtle to maintain focus on the content.
- Use interaction to reinforce discoverability rather than decoration.

## Example

**Name:** Scroll Reveal Animation

**URL:** Various

**Screenshot:** scroll-reveal.png

### Problem Solved

Guides visitors through the page by gradually introducing content as they scroll, improving readability and maintaining engagement.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Fade and slight upward movement.
- Consistent animation timing.
- Sections animate only once.
- Motion supports the reading flow.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Reveal major homepage sections as visitors scroll.
- Keep animations quick and understated.
- Prioritize readability over visual effects.

## Example

**Name:** Mobile Navigation

**URL:** Various

**Screenshot:** mobile-navigation.png

### Problem Solved

Provides a simple and intuitive navigation experience on smaller screens without overwhelming visitors.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Clean fullscreen or slide-out menu.
- Large tap targets.
- Clear hierarchy.
- Easy-to-find primary CTA.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Design a clean mobile menu that is easy to navigate.
- Keep the Donate or Join the Mission CTA visible within the menu.
- Prioritize accessibility and ease of use across all devices.
