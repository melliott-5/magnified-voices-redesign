## Example

**Name:** Equalize

**URL:** https://equalize.webflow.io

**Screenshot:** equalize-header-font-and-body-font.png

### Problem Solved

Creates a modern and approachable reading experience by establishing a clear visual hierarchy between headlines and supporting content while reinforcing the organization's brand personality.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Large editorial-style headlines create a confident first impression.
- Clean sans-serif typography improves readability across all screen sizes.
- Strong contrast between headings and body copy helps visitors scan content quickly.
- Consistent spacing between headlines, paragraphs, and CTAs creates a polished reading experience.
- Typography feels approachable without sacrificing professionalism.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Use bold, confident headlines to communicate the organization's mission.
- Establish a consistent typography scale across the entire website.
- Keep body copy highly readable with generous line spacing and comfortable line lengths.
- Let typography reinforce the organization's welcoming and community-focused personality.

## Example

**Name:** Giveza

**URL:** https://giveza-template.webflow.io

**Screenshot:** screencapture-giveza-template-webflow-io-2026-07-22-19_02_49.png

### Problem Solved

Uses typography to clearly organize information, making the page easy to scan while reinforcing a clean and trustworthy visual identity.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Small uppercase labels help introduce new sections without competing with primary headings.
- Large headlines establish a strong visual hierarchy.
- Simple body typography keeps longer content comfortable to read.
- Consistent font weights create rhythm throughout the page.
- Typography works together with whitespace to improve readability.

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Consider using subtle uppercase section labels to organize content.
- Use large headlines to emphasize key messages and mission statements.
- Keep supporting copy concise and highly readable.
- Maintain a consistent typography system across all pages.
