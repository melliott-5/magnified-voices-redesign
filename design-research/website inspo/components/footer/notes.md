# Footer Design Research Notes

---

## Example

**Name:** Givara Foundation Footer

**URL:** https://givara-foundation.webflow.io/#home-events

**Screenshot:** givara-footer.png

### Problem Solved

Reinforces the organization's identity while giving visitors quick access to navigation, contact information, and opportunities to stay connected.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Strong brand presence through logo placement
- Clear organization of navigation and contact information
- Newsletter signup integrated naturally into the footer
- Subtle background graphics add visual interest without distracting from the content
- Clean spacing and layout improve readability

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Reinforce the mission with the logo and a short brand statement.
- Include quick links, contact information, and social media in one organized layout.
- Provide a simple newsletter signup as a secondary conversion opportunity.

## Example

**Name:** God's Grace Footer

**URL:** https://godsgrace-template.webflow.io/

**Screenshot:** godsgrace-footer.png

### Problem Solved

Ends the website with a strong brand impression while providing visitors with essential navigation and contact information.

### Design Patterns to Keep

- Centered logo creates a strong visual anchor
- Short brand statement reinforces the organization's mission
- Simple navigation keeps important pages easy to find
- Social media links remain visible and accessible
- Balanced layout creates a polished, trustworthy appearance

### Apply to Magnified Voices

- Center the logo and reinforce the mission with a concise statement.
- Include essential navigation, contact details, and social links.
- Keep the footer clean and uncluttered to maintain a professional finish.
