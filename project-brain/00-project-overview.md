# Project Overview

## Project

Magnified Voices Website Redesign

## Organization

Magnified Voices is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering communities through education, advocacy, civic engagement, and access to resources.

## Project Goal

Redesign the current website into a modern, accessible, and community-focused experience that clearly communicates the organization's mission, demonstrates measurable impact, and encourages visitors to take action.

## Deliverables

Phase 1 Prototype:

- Homepage
- Impact page

Future Expansion:

- About
- Programs
- Resources
- Get Involved
- Contact

## Success Criteria

A first-time visitor should understand:

- Who Magnified Voices is
- What they do
- Why it matters
- How to get involved
- Why they can be trusted
