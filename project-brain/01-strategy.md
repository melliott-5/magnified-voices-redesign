# Strategy

## Current Problems

The existing website:

- Does not clearly communicate the organization's mission.
- Does not quickly explain who the organization serves.
- Lacks measurable impact throughout the experience.
- Has weak calls to action.
- Feels visually outdated.
- Does not effectively build trust.

## Opportunity

Transform the website into a storytelling platform that inspires action while making community resources easy to access.

## Primary Objectives

- Increase volunteer engagement
- Increase partnerships
- Increase donations
- Increase awareness
- Build credibility
- Connect visitors with resources

## Core Message

Empowering communities through knowledge and action.
