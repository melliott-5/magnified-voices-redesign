# Design Decisions

## Hero

Decision:
Use a bold community image with a concise value proposition rather than a collage.

Reason:
The original website lacked immediate clarity and emotional impact.

---

## CTA Hierarchy

Primary:
Join the Mission

Secondary:
Donate

Reason:
The organization's long-term success depends on growing its community, not only receiving donations.

---

## Impact

Decision:
Place measurable outcomes before asking for donations.

Reason:
Trust should be established before requesting support.
